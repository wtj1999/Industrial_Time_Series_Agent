## 最终生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand.
Design a NEW logo identity for a Chinese industrial time series AI agent software platform. User wants minimalist yet technological, dislikes both a basic hexagon-with-wave icon and ornate 3D metallic glowing sci-fi emblems. Explore a fundamentally DIFFERENT design language: sophisticated flat computational geometry, a restrained contemporary data technology brand.

Core symbol concept "signal slices": FIVE substantial vertical precision-cut bars arranged as a compact distinctive parametric glyph. Each bar consists of TWO separated aligned solid segments. The small gaps between segments rise and fall across bars, so that a single elegant continuous time-series wave is suggested entirely by NEGATIVE SPACE passing through the bars. The outer envelope is compact and optically balanced, taller at center, slightly shorter at edges, with a subtle forward progression. The fifth bar terminates with ONE detached tiny cyan square data sample at upper-right. Bold clean silhouette; mostly squared ends with tiny corner radii, precise consistent spacing. This is a bespoke memorable logo, not a generic equalizer: the carved continuous negative-space signal and asymmetrical calculated rhythm are the defining details. Use only 5 bars and one dot, do not add extra decorative detail. Visually evokes industrial sensor data, sampling, computation and intelligent inference. Design must work in monochrome and at small scale.

Visual style: utterly flat vector-like design, cobalt #2B50EC main symbol, cyan #10B9CA ONLY as a small accent sample. No gradients, no 3D, no bevel, no metal, no glow, no shadows, no hexagon, no enclosing frame, no rings, no shields, no circuits, no brain, no robot, no ECG heart-monitor spikes, no sparkles, no background patterns.

Deliver a polished landscape brand concept sheet on clean warm-white #F8FAFC. Typography elegant and clear, not ultra-bold. Generous whitespace. No other text. All examples MUST preserve the exact same five-bar negative-space glyph. Keep focus on an excellent minimalist logo, quiet technical sophistication, premium enterprise software brand rather than science-fiction imagery.
