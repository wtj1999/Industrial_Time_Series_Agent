## 图形生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: primary standalone logo symbol for an existing Chinese industrial time series multi-agent analytics platform, for web app header, favicon and presentations.
Primary request: Design a polished, original, extremely simple professional logo. Visual idea "temporal signal core": a bold open precision hexagonal enclosure, softly chamfered mechanical geometry with a continuous angular time-series waveform integrated into its center and structurally connected to the enclosure, plus ONE small detached sampling dot at the upper right gap. The mark should feel like one coherent clever geometric glyph, subtly suggesting a forward-moving S signal and an analytical industrial core. Readable at 24px. Strong silhouette, uniform substantial stroke widths, meticulously balanced negative space, restrained Swiss technology brand quality.
Color palette: main cobalt blue #2448F5 with a small ice blue #729BFF sampling dot; flat solid fills, no gradients.
Composition: one symbol only centered, occupying about 70 percent of square canvas, generous clear space. Real transparent background.
Constraints: no text, no letters, no wordmark, no gear teeth, no factory silhouette, no robot head, no brain, no circuit spaghetti, no ECG medical cross, no generic AI sparkles, no 3D, no glow, no shadows, no mockup, no border or presentation panel. Flat crisp vector-like raster edges. Strong simple designed identity, not clip art.
